A practical upward sequence

Rather than choosing one forever-role, use the next move to create stronger evidence for the move after it.

0–6 months

Land at the intersection

  • Target AI Solutions Consultant, Solutions Engineer or technical GTM roles.
  • Prefer AI, robotics, industrial technology or MedTech.
  • Package 3 public case studies before applying broadly.
6–18 months

Become the builder

  • Own automations, APIs, AI workflows and internal tools.
  • Quantify revenue, time saved, conversion or adoption impact.
  • Build a visible GTM-engineering portfolio.
18–36 months

Own the system

  • Move toward GTM Engineering Lead, AI Transformation Lead or Technical GTM Lead.
  • Own roadmap and cross-functional priorities.
  • Shift from executing projects to designing the operating model.
Key decision: if you enjoy building workflows, integrations and lightweight software, GTM Engineering has the strongest upside. If you prefer workshops, customer conversations and solution design, AI Solutions Consulting / Solutions Engineering is the cleaner immediate move.
