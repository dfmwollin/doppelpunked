FIELD GUIDE

How to play

Choose three heroes. Each has a different signature ability and ultimate. Your company must survive three giants in a row.

One round, three actions

  1. Read the giant's intent. You can see who will be hit, and for how much, before committing actions.
  2. Tap a hero and choose an ability. Every living hero acts once per round, in any order.
  3. Manage Courage. Strike earns +1, Guard earns +2, signature skills cost 2, and each hero's ultimate costs 6 (once per battle).
  4. Face the giant. Press the end-turn button to let the enemy execute its announced move. Shields absorb damage first.

Between battles

Wounded heroes recover some health. Choose one lasting blessing or supply upgrade before the next giant. Fallen heroes return at partial health between chapters.

The combat and story are imaginative fiction inspired by biblical names, not a literal reconstruction of Scripture. Progress saves automatically in this browser.
